Stuffed Grape Leaves

tender grape vine leaves rolled around a savory filling of rice, herbs, and sometimes meat, then gently simmered in a lemony broth

below is the ingredient needed for this recipe:

  • 1 cup uncooked rice
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh mint
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 jar grape leaves (about 60 leaves)

And here is the instructions:

  1. Rinse the grape leaves under cold water and remove any stems.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the rice, parsley, mint, onion, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
  3. Place a grape leaf on a flat surface, vein side up. Spoon about 1 tablespoon of the rice mixture onto the center of the leaf.
  4. Fold in the sides of the leaf and roll it up tightly to enclose the filling. Repeat with the remaining leaves and filling.
  5. Place the stuffed grape leaves seam side down in a large pot. Add enough water to cover them and place a plate on top to keep them submerged.
  6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for about 45 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and the leaves are tender.
  7. Remove from heat and let cool slightly before serving. Serve warm or at room temperature with lemon wedges.
