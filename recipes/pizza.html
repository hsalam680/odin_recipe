Pizza Margarita

Pizza is a popular Italian dish that consists of a round, flat base of dough topped with various ingredients such as tomato sauce, cheese, and toppings like vegetables, meats, and herbs. It is typically baked in an oven until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbly. Pizza comes in many different styles and flavors, making it a versatile and beloved food enjoyed by people all over the world.

below is the ingredient needed for this recipe:

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water (110°F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 8 ounces fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil for drizzling

And here is the instructions:

  1. In a small bowl, combine warm water and yeast. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour, olive oil, salt, and sugar. Add the yeast mixture and knead the dough until smooth and elastic.
  3. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until it doubles in size.
  4. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven to preheat as well.
  5. Once the dough has risen, punch it down and roll it out on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  6. Transfer the rolled-out dough onto a pizza peel or baking sheet. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  7. Arrange slices of fresh mozzarella cheese on top of the sauce. Add fresh basil leaves for flavor.
  8. Drizzle olive oil over the pizza for added richness.
  9. If using a pizza stone, carefully transfer the pizza onto the preheated stone in the oven. If using a baking sheet, place it directly in the oven.
  10. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  11. Remove from the oven and let it cool slightly before slicing and serving. Enjoy your homemade Pizza Margarita!
